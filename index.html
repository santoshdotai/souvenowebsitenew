When all three work together

Souveno is not three separate products.

Communication AI tells Souveno what people are saying. Vision AI tells Souveno what is physically happening. Workflow AI connects both to business action. That connection is the Souveno Intelligent Business OS.

  1. Truck enters factory.
  2. Vision AI detects vehicle arrival.
  3. Workflow AI checks dispatch and order information.
  4. ERP or database provides customer and order data.
  5. Loading starts, completes and becomes an operational event.
  6. Workflow updates dispatch status.
  7. Communication AI sends customer update.
  8. Management dashboard records turnaround.
