Which domain should customers use?

Indian customers should use Souveno.in as the working product site. Souveno.ai is the information and global roadmap site.

Does Souveno replace WhatsApp?

No. Souveno works on top of WhatsApp Business so customers continue messaging normally while the business gets automation and visibility.

What languages are supported?

The India product focuses on English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali and more, including voice-note understanding.

Can my team reply manually?

Yes. Human takeover is part of the workflow, especially for special pricing, approvals, discounts and sensitive customers.

Will Souveno go global?

Yes. India is the first market. Souveno.ai will explain the global roadmap as the product expands beyond India.
