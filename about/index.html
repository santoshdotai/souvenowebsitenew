About Souveno

We connect business information to action.

Souveno builds Intelligent Business Operating Systems that bring Communication AI, Workflow AI and Vision AI together.

We work on the everyday gaps between conversations, documents, business software and physical operations. Our purpose is to help organisations turn those signals into useful, accountable action.

What we build

Intelligence around the way your business works.

Understand

Structure information from messages, documents and operational events.

Decide

Bring together business rules, relevant context and human review.

Act

Connect approved actions to existing systems and track what happens next.

How we work

Start with one problem. Expand after proof.

We begin by understanding the operation and identifying a measurable bottleneck. We design an appropriate solution, prove it in a controlled pilot, integrate it into the workflow and measure the result.

Our approach combines applied AI, automation, computer vision and conventional software according to the problem. Human approvals and clear records belong in the workflow wherever judgement or accountability is required.
