Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Souveno is currently collecting pilot, demo, and go-live interest from businesses that want to automate WhatsApp enquiry to quote workflows.

Information we collect

When you submit a form, we may collect your name, business name, WhatsApp number, email address, business type, and any message or workflow details you choose to share.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to pilot applications, schedule demos, understand your quote workflow, provide onboarding support, and send product updates you requested.

Website enquiries and referral information

The use-case form prepares a WhatsApp message containing your name, business, workflow and answer to “How did you hear about us?”. These details are shared with Souveno when you choose to send the message in WhatsApp. The website does not claim an enquiry has been delivered when you only open WhatsApp. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy policy.

Optional website analytics

When enabled and with your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures page visits and contact-button interactions. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and session recordings to help us improve the website. We mask the enquiry form in Clarity and do not send your name, business name or free-text enquiry in our custom analytics events. We measure an enquiry handoff separately from a confirmed lead or booked call.

You can allow or decline optional analytics and change your choice through “Analytics preferences” when analytics is enabled. Your preference is stored in your browser. Essential contact links work without analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may use trusted service providers to receive form submissions, host the website, or operate email and communication workflows.

Retention

We keep pilot and contact details only as long as needed for business follow-up, product onboarding, legal, or operational purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your submitted details by contacting Souveno through the website contact flow.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information, but no online system is completely secure. Do not submit sensitive financial passwords or confidential customer lists through public forms.
